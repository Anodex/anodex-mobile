What each one commits you to

  • A is nearly free and I'd ship it whatever else you pick. It is one composable and no new tokens.
  • The facet pip costs nothing either, and it is the single cheapest thing that makes the app look like the rest of Anodex — the hexagon is already the house shape in AnodexIcon.
  • The arrival spine is the one with a rule attached. The cyan is reserved for rare "arrival" moments in the desktop's own palette notes, so it earns its place only if it fires once when a reply lands and never loops. An ambient shimmer down the side of every message would be exactly the thing we said not to build.
  • The facet field behind the thread is the one I am least sure of. It works on the empty screen because there is nothing to read. Behind text it risks being noise you cannot switch off — worth trying at 3–4% and cutting if it bothers the eye.
  • Panel B also shows two things already shipped in 0.27: the pinned current request, and a per-message persona — note Rook answering under Vale's earlier turn, each keeping its own name and colour.
